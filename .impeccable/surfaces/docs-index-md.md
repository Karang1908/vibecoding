## Scope and mode

Read mode for the workshop overview, active lesson path, setup material, and post-workshop reference pages.

## Audience and job

First-time builders follow the site during a projected, hands-on lab and revisit it later on a laptop or phone. They need to recognize the current session, understand one idea at a time, and find the next action without scanning a dense handbook.

## Action and proof

The primary action is to begin or continue the current lesson. The path proves progress through three concrete outcomes: a first webpage, a saved project, and a deployed app with a real database.

## Direction

A Google Codelabs-style learning path with the real GDG BITS Pilani Dubai Campus identity. The memorable moment is the blue step rail carrying the student from an idea to a live URL.

## Constraints

Keep workshop copy concise and casual. Preserve the active curriculum sequence. Use supplied logos without redrawing them. Reflow cleanly for phones, browser zoom, and projection. Do not invent social proof or event outcomes.
